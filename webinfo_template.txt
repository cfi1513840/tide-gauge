Welcome to the tide station. It is part of a free, volunteer-run monitoring service built and maintained by community members and academic partners along the coast.

Real-time tide levels, weather conditions, and predictive alerts are provided at no cost to mariners, fishermen, harbor operators, and anyone who depends on accurate, up-to-date coastal information.

Explore current and predicted tidal conditions, review historical trends, or sign up for personalized alerts delivered straight to your email or phone.
