Twilio.com provides the SMS services used by the tide station to
issue text messages for administrative and alerting purposes. A new
tide station deployment would normally use an existing Twilio
account and sending phone number.

If this deployment requires setting up a new Twilio account instead,
refer to Twilio's own documentation at https://www.twilio.com/docs
for up to date guidance on that process, including selection and
verification of a Twilio sending phone number.

The Twilio account credentials must be entered into the
tide_constants.json file. For an existing account, the credentials
will be provided by the tide station network administrator; for a
new account, they are derived from the account settings following
configuration and testing.

    Account SID (Twilio home page)          TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID
    Auth Token (Twilio home page)           TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN
    Phone number (Twilio My Inventory page) TWILIO_PHONE_SENDER

TWILIO_PHONE_RECIPIENT is not taken from Twilio at all -- it is
simply the phone number assigned to this station's own administrator.
